## 中文版 (Chinese Version)

本隐私政策适用于由开发团队（以下简称“我们”）开发和分发的移动应用程序《楞严》（以下简称“本应用”）。我们深知个人隐私对您的重要性，并致力于保护您的个人信息安全。

### 1. 核心原则：零数据收集
《楞严》是一款完全本地化运行的经文阅读与禅修应用。本应用的核心设计哲学是提供一个纯净、无打扰的阅读环境。
- **不收集任何个人数据**：我们不收集、不记录、不请求任何您的个人身份信息（如姓名、电子邮件地址、电话号码、设备唯一标识符等）。
- **完全本地运行**：您的阅读进度、收藏记录、播放历史以及偏好设置均仅存储在您本人的 iOS 设备本地（使用系统内置的 `UserDefaults` 和本地沙盒存储）。我们没有服务器，因此无法并且绝不传输或上传这些数据。

### 2. 权限说明
本应用可能会在必要时请求以下系统权限，其目的仅限于实现特定的本地功能：
- **音频播放**：用于在后台播放经文音频。
- **触觉反馈**：用于在您点击书签或交互时提供本地震动反馈（由系统 Taptic Engine 提供，完全在本地处理）。
- **本应用绝不请求**：相机、麦克风、地理位置、通讯录或任何与广告追踪相关的权限。

### 3. 第三方服务
本应用中不包含任何第三方广告 SDK、数据分析 SDK 或追踪工具（如 Google Analytics、Flurry 等），绝不存在任何后台数据分析或用户画像收集行为。

### 4. 政策更新
我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何更新都将在此页面公布，由于本应用完全本地化运行，我们无法主动向您推送隐私政策更新通知，请您定期查看本页面以获取最新信息。

## English Version

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "LengYan" (hereinafter referred to as the "App"), developed and distributed by the development team (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our"). We understand the importance of personal privacy and are committed to protecting the security of your personal information.

### 1. Core Principle: Zero Data Collection
"LengYan" is a scripture reading and meditation app that runs entirely locally on your device. The core design philosophy of this App is to provide a pure, distraction-free reading environment.
- **No Personal Data Collected**: We do not collect, record, or request any of your personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, phone number, unique device identifier, etc.).
- **100% Local Execution**: Your reading progress, bookmarks, playback history, and preferences are stored exclusively on your own iOS device (using the built-in system `UserDefaults` and local sandbox storage). We do not operate any backend servers; therefore, we cannot and will never transmit or upload this data.

### 2. Device Permissions
The App may request the following system permissions when necessary, solely for the purpose of implementing specific local features:
- **Audio Playback**: Used to support background audio playback of scripture media.
- **Haptic Feedback**: Used to provide subtle vibration feedback (powered by the system's Taptic Engine) when you bookmark pages or interact with UI elements, processed entirely locally.
- **We Never Request**: Access to your camera, microphone, location services, contacts, or any advertising tracking permissions.

### 3. Third-Party Services
This App does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking tools (such as Google Analytics, Flurry, etc.). There is absolutely no background data analytics or user profiling behavior.

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Since the App runs entirely locally, we cannot proactively notify you of updates. Please review this page periodically for any changes.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please feel free to contact us:
