# 《楞严》隐私政策 / LengYan Privacy Policy

*更新日期 / Last updated: 2026 年 7 月 18 日 / July 18, 2026*

## 中文

### 日常使用

阅读进度、书签、收藏、播放进度和显示设置仅保存在设备本地。App 不含广告、分析或跨应用追踪。

### 主动反馈

提交反馈时，我们会收到反馈内容和提交时间；App 内反馈还包含 App 版本号。这些信息仅用于处理反馈和改进 App。

反馈将在 30 天内删除。如需提前删除，可通过[支持页](https://xuan9.github.io/lengyan/)联系我们。

---

## English

### Everyday use

Reading progress, bookmarks, favorites, playback progress, and display settings stay on your device. The App contains no ads, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

### Feedback you choose to send

When you send feedback, we receive its content and submission time. Feedback sent from the App also includes the App version. We use this information only to handle feedback and improve the App.

Feedback is deleted within 30 days. To request earlier deletion, contact us through the [support page](https://xuan9.github.io/lengyan/).
